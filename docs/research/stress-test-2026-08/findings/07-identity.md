## FINDING 24: Two IS_TYPE_OF parents make inheritance merge and LCA widening undefined, breaking ADR-0023 convergence
**Severity claimed:** high · **Verdict:** PARTIAL

### Scenario
The telecom community asserts Smartphone IS_TYPE_OF Mobile Phone; the computing community asserts Smartphone IS_TYPE_OF Portable Computer. Both edges are true, nothing forbids the second (ARCHITECTURE calls the taxonomy a 'tree' but no mechanism enforces single-parent), and ADR-0023 order-independence guarantees both eventually exist. Mobile Phone's family edges include → Cellular Radio and a requirement expression OR(GSM-stack, CDMA-stack); Portable Computer's include → Processor and → Operating System. Later, the x86-prototype-style exception fires on a smartphone instance and a moderator must WIDEN a violated requirement to 'the' LCA.

### Claimed failure
ADR-0019 defines effective dependencies as 'inherited family edges + own edges' for ONE family; with two parents the union produces duplicate role requirements at different granularity (Mobile Phone → Mobile Processor AND Computer → Processor: the same physical slot required twice, and ADR-0021 shadowing cannot dedupe them because they are distinct edges on distinct nodes — BOM counts double). ADR-0017 gives no merge semantics for two parents' requirement expression trees ('getRecursiveRequirements merge' in ARCHITECTURE §2 is named, never defined for multi-parent). And ADR-0008's widening presumes a tree: in a DAG the least common ancestor is not unique (Processor via the computer lineage vs. Radio-Equipped Device via the phone lineage are incomparable common ancestors), yet WIDEN stores exactly one relaxed_target_id — two moderators legally pick different LCAs and the graph does NOT converge to the same state, contradicting ADR-0023's pinned invariant. TB-017/018/034 all exercise single-parent inheritance only; Q-14 covers causality disputes, not taxonomy-placement arbitration.

### Verifier adjudication (full)
Most of the claimed breakage dissolves on inspection, but one rule is genuinely unwritten. Refutations first. (1) Poly-hierarchy is intended, not accidental: the design digest (docs/archive/digests/2026-01-18-gemini-long-chat.md) records that the taxonomy was made edge-based precisely 'for poly-hierarchy and no sync bugs' — ARCHITECTURE.md's word 'tree' is loose phrasing, not a constraint. (2) Convergence (ADR-0023) is NOT contradicted: the invariant is that any ORDER of the same true additions converges; two moderators recording different relaxed_target_ids are different additions (different override facts), the same class as TB-039's virus-vs-disease misplacement ('ordinary technically-true-adjacent granularity, healed by refinement + linter, never a catastrophe') and the 802.11 example's ruling that judgment calls are 'editorial, not structural — disputes land in the normal moderation lane (Q-14).' Meanwhile the mechanical merge IS order-independent, because union of inherited edges (ADR-0019 §1: 'own edges ∪ inherited edges − exclusions' — nothing restricts it to one parent) and AND-composition (ADR-0017's default: unreferenced edges are 'implicitly ANDed in') are commutative. (3) The widening scenario is malformed: WIDEN relaxes the requirement's PROVIDER target to a common ancestor of the original target and the actual exceptional provider (TB-017: Mobile Processor vs the x86 chip → Processor); 'Radio-Equipped Device' is an ancestor of the smartphone (consumer side) and is never a candidate relaxed target, so the posed ambiguity does not arise. Even under genuine provider-side non-uniqueness, every common ancestor is truth-preserving (wider = less resolution, ADR-0015), the override carries justification as editable content, and ADR-0017's OR can record both — no wrongness is expressible. (4) Duplicate role at two granularities: both inherited requirements are TRUE of a smartphone (it does require a Processor, and a Mobile Processor), so this is redundant truth, and the documented solve-time merge is 'inherit and TIGHTEN' (ARCHITECTURE §2, getRecursiveRequirements) — Mobile Processor IS_TYPE_OF Processor makes the finer requirement subsume the coarser; existence queries are unaffected (one provider satisfies both leaves), and BOM double-counting lands squarely in the deliberately deferred quantities area (Q-10, TB-022 accepted-open) plus ADR-0021's 'fully covers' definition, which is explicitly solver-phase work for single-parent cases too. What is genuinely missing — the PARTIAL: no ADR states the multi-parent composition rule. The needed rule, precisely: 'IS_TYPE_OF poly-hierarchy is legal (fix ARCHITECTURE's tree wording to DAG); a node's effective requirements compose across ALL taxonomy parents as the AND of each parent's inherited requirement expression, unioned with own edges, with same-role requirements at different granularity deduped by tightening when related by IS_TYPE_OF; and ADR-0008/0019's WIDEN target is any common ancestor of original target and exceptional provider — the choice among incomparable minimal ancestors is editorial content in the Q-14 lane, safe because every choice is truth-preserving.' That is a one-paragraph extension of ADR-0019, not new machinery — but until it is written, 'the LCA' and the one-family reading of ADR-0019 are honest textual gaps, and getRecursiveRequirements' multi-parent behavior rests on inference rather than a documented decision.

## FINDING 23: iPhone 15 Pro generation-by-trim matrix has no legal attachment point
**Severity claimed:** high · **Verdict:** HANDLED

### Scenario
iPhone 15 earns a version node (USB-C, a genuinely new dependency via EU legislation — Bridge). iPhone 15 Pro / 15 Pro Max are minor variants OF iPhone 15, so per ADR-0009 they start as ProductIteration records. Question: which node holds those records, and what happens when 15 Pro Max later earns a node (periscope telephoto — Bridge into folded optics)? Also: where does 'A17 Pro chip' attach — a truth of exactly the 15-generation Pro trims (not iPhone 15 base, not iPhone 14 Pro, not the family)?

### Claimed failure
ADR-0018 §1 and ADR-0009 place iteration records on THE family/series root — but a 15-Pro-Max record on the iPhone root loses its generation binding, and lifting it (ADR-0018 §4) must produce IS_REFINEMENT_OF → root (flat star, §3), severing inheritance of iPhone 15's own dependencies. The alternative — records on iPhone 15, making it both a version node and a series root — means lifting creates IS_REFINEMENT_OF → iPhone 15, an explicit chain that ADR-0018 §3 forbids ('never chained'), even though dragging iPhone 15's requirements down the chain is precisely the inheritance needed here. The ADR-0020 sub-family patch (iPhone Pro IS_TYPE_OF iPhone) covers trim-wide truths but the generation×trim INTERSECTION ('A17 Pro: gen-15 Pros only') has no legal home at all: root, iPhone 15, and iPhone Pro all over- or under-claim, and TB-035's worked example silently assumes '15 Pro Max: instance-level edge' without saying what its parents are. Bonus collision: ADR-0022 §4's identity-container end state ('root holds NOTHING but name, succession, story edges') is unreachable while ADR-0018 §1 still parks record-only iterations on that same root, and no ADR defines a record-migration operation (only record→edge lifting exists).

### Verifier adjudication (full)
Each 'contradiction' rests on a misreading of an ADR's scope. (1) 'Never chained' (ADR-0018 §3): the rule's stated target is version-to-version SUCCESSION chains — the worked example spells it out: 'Chaining (ax → n → g → root) would make requirement inheritance drag version-specific baggage down the chain; succession is story, told with dated REPLACED_BY edges.' iPhone 15 Pro Max is not a successor of iPhone 15; it is a parallel sub-line member, and the design has a dedicated, already-worked device for exactly that shape: the sub-family (iPhone Pro IS_TYPE_OF iPhone, ADR-0020 §2) plus first-class instance nodes connected via IS_TYPE_OF (ADR-0001, Glossary 'Instance'). When 15 Pro Max earns its node (periscope Bridge), it lifts as an Artifact instance: IS_TYPE_OF iPhone Pro, with its own edges to its specific truths (periscope module, A17 Pro) per ADR-0008/ADR-0018 §5 ('historical instances link to the version they actually used'). No IS_REFINEMENT_OF chain through iPhone 15 is needed, so no §3 violation arises. (2) 'Severed inheritance of iPhone 15's dependencies': the generation truth the trims must inherit (USB-C from 2023) is CONTIGUOUS, and ADR-0019 §4's rule for contiguous eras is a dated family edge ('USB-C IS_COMPONENT_OF iPhone, start 2023.7') — every post-2023 instance inherits it as a presumption through the ordinary family channel, no chain required. The iPhone 15 version node exists for what is true of exactly that generation; instances reach generation truths via dated family edges or their own asserted edges, which is TB-034's solved machinery. (3) The A17 Pro generation×trim intersection: the claim that root/iPhone 15/iPhone Pro 'all over- or under-claim' is a false trilemma, because truth-granular attachment (ADR-0018 §4, TB-033) never requires a single node whose extension equals the truth set — it explicitly attaches 'edges to exactly the versions that have them (non-contiguous presence naturally supported),' and below node granularity, iteration-record tech-links, lifting monotonically. A17 Pro gets edges/record-links to exactly {15 Pro, 15 Pro Max}. An 'iPhone 15 Pro' intersection abstraction is available lazily if a second shared trait ever forces it (ADR-0008), but is never required — this is the same shape as TSF-on-{ac,ax,be} in the 802.11 example §3. That also answers 'TB-035's instance-level edge without parents': the parents are the ADR-0020 taxonomy (IS_TYPE_OF iPhone Pro), stated in the same example that introduces the sub-family. (4) Record placement and generation binding: ProductIteration records are structured data (name, year, key feature, optional tech links — ADR-0009), not nodes; generation membership is record data and loses nothing sitting on the family root. (5) The ADR-0022 §4 'collision': the identity container is 'a legal, honest end state' — MAY degrade, never MUST — so a root still holding records contradicts nothing; and §4 itself names the migration path ('family-wide claims get... pushed down to generation nodes'). Moving a record between nodes is an ordinary versioned data edit (ADR-0011), not a missing schema operation; the one operation genuinely needed, record→node+edges lifting, is already an acknowledged deliverable in ADR-0018's consequences.

## FINDING 25: Qualifier homonym fork breaks ADR-0028's zero-information-loss split escape hatch
**Severity claimed:** medium · **Verdict:** HANDLED

### Scenario
The software-history community uses SUCCEEDS(qualifier: 'forked') meaning continuation-with-shared-code (OpenOffice → LibreOffice, per the product-morphing example). The standards community starts tagging incompatible spec schisms 'forked' (e.g., a fieldbus standard splitting into two rival incompatible specs where NEITHER is the successor). The ADR-0004-pattern canonicalizer — built to collapse 'very flat' → Surface_Roughness_RMS — dutifully funnels both communities' free text onto the single slug 'forked'. Years later, story-traversal profiling triggers ADR-0028's promised escape hatch: split ASSOCIATION/SUCCEEDS mechanically by qualifier.

### Claimed failure
ADR-0028 rests on 'type = f(qualifier), zero information loss' — but a homonym slug means one qualifier value needs TWO destination partitions, so the promised mechanical migration is neither mechanical nor lossless; author intent was destroyed at canonicalization time and is unrecoverable (qualifiers are bare slugs, not versioned entities — ADR-0011's merge-redirect/rollback machinery covers nodes and edges, not vocabulary). TB-032 pins an explicit asymmetry for NODES ('never auto-merge distinct things — that would be wrong'), but ADR-0024 §4 gives the qualifier vocabulary the OPPOSITE bias (LLM-canonicalized merging to fight synonym drift) with no homonym safeguard, so the one place the design mandates eager auto-merging is exactly a place where a wrong merge is wrongness, not incompleteness (ADR-0015), and the secondary qualifier index (ADR-0024) serves semantically mixed, wrong result sets in the meantime.

### Verifier adjudication (full)
Walk the scenario through the machinery. (1) No wrong machine behavior is possible: ADR-0024's partition test makes qualifiers machine-invisible by construction — a distinction earns a TYPE iff a traverser prunes on it or a machine consumer (solver, inheritance, counting) treats it differently; everything else 'is payload'. Per ADR-0028's semantics table, nothing reads 'forked' (only 'replaced' implies usage-shift, and TB-039 shows machine-meaningful qualifiers like 'inhibits' are explicitly *reserved*). So both communities' SUCCEEDS(forked) edges assert the same graph-relevant proposition — 'B split off from A, dated' — which is TRUE in both senses (the fieldbus rivals did each fork from the parent spec, exactly as LibreOffice did). (2) ADR-0015 litmus: the coarse slug omits the compatible-vs-schism nuance, it asserts nothing false — this is incompleteness, not wrongness. Sharing a label is not merging entities: no identity claim between the two edge populations exists, so TB-032's never-auto-merge rule (about asserting two distinct THINGS are one) is not violated; the mixed secondary-index result is a flavor search truthfully returning everything labeled 'forked', the same behavior as the English word itself. (3) 'Unrecoverable author intent' is factually wrong on this design: qualifiers live on DependencyEdges, which ARE versioned entities (ADR-0011: version_uuid/previous_version_id/author/change_summary 'on every entity from day one') carrying justification and source_urls; and in this exact scenario the two populations separate mechanically by endpoint category (STANDARD_UNIT schism edges vs software-technology fork edges). (4) The escape hatch survives: ADR-0028 promises losslessness of the split BY STORED QUALIFIER — type=f(qualifier) sends all 'forked' edges to one partition, losing zero stored information. The finding silently substitutes a different operation (split by a meaning finer than the stored vocabulary), which was never promised; and by ADR-0024 rule 5, any partition-level need for that finer distinction requires an ADR first — which is precisely where the qualifier sub-splits via ADR-0004 Lazy Split ('start coarse; splitting later is easy') using the Q-04 LLM-triage pattern over provenance, the same resolution-increase move as TB-045's decomposition bounty. Coarse-first vocabulary is the blessed default because its failure mode is provably only incompleteness.

## FINDING 26: Capability routers plus the widening ratchet produce era-erasing hubs and false earliest-possible answers
**Severity claimed:** medium · **Verdict:** HANDLED

### Scenario
'High-Performance Computing' (named as a router in the 802.11 example §5) accumulates providers from UNIVAC (1951) to exascale GPU clusters (2026). A contributor lazily links a 2025 'frontier-model training run' node ENABLES ← High-Performance Computing with no edge constraints — explicitly legal (ADR-0017: absent tree = AND, 'lazy authoring stays legal'; ADR-0005 constraints are consumer-optional). A user asks the signature query: earliest-possible-date for frontier-model training.

### Claimed failure
Earliest-possible-date = MAX over hard deps (ARCHITECTURE §6) resolves the router at its earliest satisfying provider and answers ~1951 — a false possibility, i.e., wrongness, violating ADR-0015's prime directive, because missing CONSTRAINTS fail over-permissive while missing EDGES fail under-permissive: the monotonicity story ('adding information only unlocks') is inverted for constraint pruning and no ADR acknowledges it. Two mechanisms actively feed the failure: (1) gatekeeper nodes (ADR-0003) encode era boundaries topologically, but capability routers (ADR-0018 §7) exist precisely to FLATTEN topology — the design never says which pattern a domain gets, and a router is a gatekeeper bypass; (2) ADR-0008 widening is a one-way ratchet — every historical exception relaxes requirements UP toward the LCA and nothing ever narrows them back, so requirement targets migrate monotonically toward the mushiest router available over decades of edits. Capability nodes are also the only node class with NO gating or splitting rule at all (Significance Filter gates products, Manufacturing Test gates materials; nothing defines when 'HPC 1950–2026' must split into era- or magnitude-bounded routers). TB-001 and TB-013 assume the seam constraints exist; neither covers the semantic-drift/false-possibility mode.

### Verifier adjudication (full)
The central claim — 'missing constraints fail over-permissive and no ADR acknowledges it' — is directly contradicted by ADR-0025 §6, which canonizes exactly this failure mode: 'an absurd answer is a diagnosis... it localizes a missing constraint, edge, or node (this is literally how the need for attribute constraints was discovered — the vacuum-tube iPhone trace)', feeding the flag-and-fix bounty loop (ADR-0003 consequences: 'Flag-and-fix becomes a gameplay loop'). The scenario IS TB-001 wearing a router hat: a technically-true path (UNIVAC satisfies an unconstrained HPC edge) answered coarsely until a seam constraint prunes it — and calling that pre-constraint answer 'wrongness' re-litigates accepted ADR-0003, whose settled position is that such paths 'lack resolution'. The monotonicity-inversion claim collapses on inspection: with NO edge, the frontier-training node is a magic-box REALIZED leaf (possible always — the explicitly blessed graceful-ignorance default, ARCHITECTURE §2); the lazy HPC edge tightens that to ≥1951; a FLOPS constraint (an ordinary addable fact, ADR-0005) tightens it to ~2020s — every fact moves the entailment monotonically toward truth, and per ADR-0026 the answer is computed-never-stored, 'exactly as good as the facts', served with a trace that self-diagnoses (1951-via-UNIVAC is a visible absurdity, not a silent one). Router-vs-gatekeeper: routers are not gatekeeper bypasses — ADR-0018 §7 defines them as bundles of RECURRING DEMAND PROFILES, i.e. constraint profiles (the 802.11 example names them with their profiles: µW power, rad-hard, perf/watt); an unconstrained edge to one is ordinary lazy coarse authoring, legal and repairable. The 'missing split rule' exists and answers NEVER: era/magnitude is an attribute, so 'HPC-1970s' nodes are the DigiKey mistake (ADR-0004 Manufacturing Test — no BOM/physics change); differentiation lives in consumer constraints evaluated against dated provider timelines intersected per ARCHITECTURE §6. The widening ratchet mischaracterizes ADR-0008: widening fires only on a JUSTIFIED real historical exception, relaxes only to the LCA of actual implementations (never 'the mushiest router available'), is recorded as a RequirementOverride, and has counter-mechanisms (constraints narrow at any time; the single-child-abstraction garbage collector collapses back). The only genuine residual — authoring-time constraint nudging so the lazy edge gets its FLOPS constraint sooner — is already tracked as open work (Q-08 Expertise Gap: LLM-suggested constraints and requirement templates; TB-013's Phase-2 Microprocessor density test), which concerns repair SPEED, not a missing correctness mechanism.
